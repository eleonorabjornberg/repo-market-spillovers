A flag says something has changed, not that a crisis is coming. The project's studies found that none of these indicators reliably warned of past stress (studies 04–06). The flags and their thresholds were fixed before the first build, in the design. Much of the growth in cleared repo since late 2023 is the market moving ahead of the clearing deadlines (study 07).
