The flags and their thresholds were fixed before the first build, in the design. Much of the growth in cleared repo since late 2023 is the market moving ahead of the clearing deadlines (study 07).

The repo still outside clearing

Primary dealers report each week how much cash they lend against Treasuries, and through which channel. In the four weeks to 14 October 2026, 55% of it was uncleared bilateral repo, against 54% in the same weeks a year earlier (+1.2 points). The SEC rule requires much of this segment to be centrally cleared by 30 June 2027; how far it reaches trades between affiliates is still contested. The share has fallen only slowly while uncleared lending kept growing in dollars, and it dips each year-end as trades are moved into clearing for the balance-sheet date and back out after it, so compare it with the same weeks a year earlier. It covers primary dealers only, and says nothing about haircuts or who the counterparty is; regulators see those, the public does not (study 14). Weeks where the New York Fed suppresses a figure are left out of the line.

Reserves against the debt

Repo got tight when the reserves banks keep at the Fed fell to about a tenth of the federal debt held by the public, in 2018–19 and again in 2025–26 (study 20). In the week of 21 October 2026 the ratio was 9.3%, the scarce zone (below 10% scarce, 11% or more ample). Whether the gauge calls the months ahead correctly is being tested, from November 2026, against predictions written down beforehand (study 21: pending).

The clearing migration still to come

About 40% of money funds' repo lending now goes through FICC's central clearing (study 07). The hedge-fund side is further behind, and the SEC requires it cleared by 30 June 2027 (246 days away). In 2026 Q2, FICC sponsored repo, mostly hedge funds borrowing cash, equalled 31% of hedge funds' repo borrowing. It is a proxy built from public data, measured differently from Form PF, so its trend means more than its level; it tracks the New York Fed's figure from non-public data (about 30% in late 2025). It jumps around each year-end, so compare a quarter with the same quarter a year earlier. A rising line is the migration happening; a flat or falling one as the deadline nears is worth watching.
